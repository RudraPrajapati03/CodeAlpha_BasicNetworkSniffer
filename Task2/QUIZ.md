# Phishing Awareness Quiz

Choose one answer for each question. Check the answer key after finishing.

## Questions

**1. An unexpected message says your account will close in 10 minutes unless you enter your password and OTP. What should you do?**

A. Enter the details quickly  
B. Reply and ask if it is real  
C. Avoid the link and verify through the official app/site or a known support channel  
D. Forward it to everyone

**2. Which is a common phishing warning sign?**

A. An expected message confirmed through a trusted channel  
B. A suspicious sender combined with urgency and an unusual request  
C. A routine internal message that matches known context  
D. A bookmarked official website

**3. Your “manager” emails asking for gift-card codes and says not to call. What is the safest response?**

A. Buy the cards immediately  
B. Send one code as a test  
C. Reply with your personal phone number  
D. Verify the request using a separate, known contact method

**4. You clicked a suspicious link and entered your password. What should you do?**

A. Report it promptly, change the password through the official site, and follow IT/security instructions  
B. Delete the email and tell nobody  
C. Reuse the password elsewhere  
D. Wait to see whether anything happens

**5. What should you do with a suspicious message at work?**

A. Post it publicly  
B. Forward it to all coworkers  
C. Report it using the approved security/IT process  
D. Click the link to investigate

## Answer key and explanations

1. **C** — Verify independently; never share passwords or one-time codes through an unexpected message.
2. **B** — Sender anomalies, urgency, and unusual requests together warrant caution.
3. **D** — Verify payment requests using a trusted channel and normal approval procedures.
4. **A** — Fast reporting helps responders limit possible harm; use the official site, not the suspicious link.
5. **C** — Use the organization's approved reporting process and preserve the message as instructed.

## Scoring

- **5/5:** Strong understanding of the core safety actions.
- **3–4/5:** Review the explanations for missed answers.
- **0–2/5:** Repeat the lesson and quiz, then discuss questions with a trainer.

This short quiz checks awareness only; it does not measure every aspect of phishing risk.
